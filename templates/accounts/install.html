Install on your phone

Pocket of Paradise works like an app: home-screen icon, full screen, stays signed in.

Scan with your phone camera

{{ site_url }}

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the link in Safari (not Chrome) and sign in.
  2. Tap the Share button {% include "components/icon.html" with name="share" size="16" %} at the bottom (top on iPad).
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Pocket of Paradise from your home screen. To get alerts, tap your avatar → Enable notifications (iOS 16.4+).

Android, Windows, Mac, ChromeOS

  1. Open the link in Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap Install app in the top bar, or the browser menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. On a Mac with Safari 17+: File → Add to Dock.
  4. Launch it from the home screen, Start menu, Dock or app launcher.
