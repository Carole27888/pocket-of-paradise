Privacy notice

How {{ company.name }} looks after your personal information.

What we collect

When you enquire or book we collect your name, phone number, e-mail, nationality, travel dates and party size. For park permits and travel documents we may also collect passport details, dietary and medical notes and an emergency contact.

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry, prepare quotations and itineraries, and run your safari.
  • To take payments and issue invoices and receipts.
  • To remind you about your trip, and to ask for your feedback afterwards.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

Who sees it

Only our staff who need it for your booking, and suppliers who must know it to serve you (lodges, park authorities, transporters). Passport numbers are stored encrypted. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

Sensitive trip details (passport, medical, emergency contact) are removed about 90 days after your trip. Messages and unsuccessful enquiries are removed after one to two years. Financial records are kept as the law requires, without your personal details once you ask us to erase them.

Your rights

You may ask to see your data, correct it, have it deleted, or object to how we use it. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).
