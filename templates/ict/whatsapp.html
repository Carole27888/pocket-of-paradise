3 · Message templates

Needed for messages outside the 24-hour window

WhatsApp lets a business send free text only within 24 hours of the person's last message. Alerts and reminders usually fall outside that, so Meta requires an approved template. The system picks the template for each kind of message and fills in the details. Create the standard set, submit it, and wait for approval (minutes to a day).
