Set-up steps (once, at developers.facebook.com)
  1. Create a Business app and add the Webhooks, Facebook Login for Business and WhatsApp products.
  2. Paste the callback URL and verify token above into the webhook settings and press Verify. Save the App secret (App settings → Basic) and a long-lived Page access token here.
  3. Subscribe Page → leadgen, messages, Instagram → messages and WhatsApp Business Account → messages. Subscribe the Page to the app.
  4. Paste each Meta campaign, ad set, ad or form ID into the campaign's Meta IDs field so leads are credited to it.
  5. Meta requires App Review for these permissions before leads from the public arrive. Test first with the Lead Ads Testing Tool.
