# Pocket of Paradise Management System

*How the system powers every department*

Pocket of Paradise Travel Company · November 2026

## Part A: Introduction

One system that runs the whole travel office, from the first enquiry to the final report.

- Enquiries, clients, trips, money, staff and marketing in one place.
- Each person sees only the work that belongs to their role.
- Follow-ups, reminders and alerts happen by themselves.
- Management sees every department live, without waiting for reports.
- Works on a computer and installs on any phone like an app.

## Part C: Standout features

### Automation: on its own

- Creates follow-ups for quotes, deposits and quiet leads
- Reassigns leads not contacted in time (if switched on)
- SMS balance and instalment reminders to clients
- Alerts for unready trips, late vehicles, KRA dates
- Monthly management pack to the directors

### Automation: on command

- Run all follow-up rules with one click
- Assign a batch of leads by the rules
- "Write for me" drafts itinerary text
- Bulk SMS and email with opt-outs
- Reports as Excel, CSV or PDF, or by email

### Lead capture and follow-up

- Desk, website, catalogue, expo form, social, QR cards
- Returning clients matched by phone or email
- Rules and fair turn-taking choose the agent
- Clear stages from Enquiry to Completed

### Website and social media

- Website enquiries land in the system
- Public tour catalogue for clients
- Facebook and Instagram leads and messages
- Tracked links show what brought each lead

*Starts once the website and Meta accounts are linked.*

### WhatsApp messages

- Welcome message to new enquiries
- Travel reminders to client and agent
- Pre-trip and thank-you messages
- Every message logged

*Sends once WhatsApp Business is connected.*

### AI assistant for Director and COO

- Ask on WhatsApp, get live figures
- "Ask the business" on the Command Centre
- Daily morning briefing
- Read-only: it can never change data

*AI wording needs an AI service connected.*

### Agent QR business cards

- Personal card, front and back
- Scan to save contact and send a trip request
- Request goes straight to that agent
- Shows scans, saves, leads and bookings

### Travel reminders and client links

- Itinerary link: Book, Change or Question
- Private trip page: paid, due, what to bring
- Travel-day page for the agent
- Vouchers and letters from the booking

### Reports and dashboards

- A dashboard for every department
- Command Centre for management
- Sales insights and market research
- Financial statements and budgets

## Part D: The result

One reliable place for every enquiry, trip, payment and person. Less chasing, faster service, and the whole business at a glance.
